How mortgage overpayments work

Every pound you overpay reduces your outstanding balance immediately, which reduces the interest charged in future months. Because mortgage interest is calculated on the remaining balance, even a small regular overpayment compounds into significant savings over the life of the loan.

For example, overpaying £200/month on a 25-year £250,000 mortgage at 4.5% saves over £26,000 in interest and cuts the mortgage by around 4 years.
